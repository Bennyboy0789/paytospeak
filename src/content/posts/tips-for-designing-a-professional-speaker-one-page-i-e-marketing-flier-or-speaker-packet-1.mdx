But first, context... from my experience, PAID speakers can be selected by a wide variety of different people, meeting planners, conference organizers, executives, HR Directors, speakers agents, bureaus, campuses, student councils, school leadership, and many more.

If the speech isn't curated with the "3 E's", a great website doesn't matter. Neither does a demo video or a One Page.

> General tips on creating a One Page:

1. Study other speaker's samples (find them online and search)
2. Expect to have a start-up version and grow into a more detailed version after getting more clients
3. Don't design it yourself unless you are a professional graphic designer.
4. Focus on content (i.e. copy text) first.
5. Have your text edited and ready, as if you're comfortable inking it on your arm as a tattoo.
6. Make your content universal so a variety of audiences find appeal and relevancy.
7. Create two versions, a standard version and a bureau-friendly version. Bureaus will not send our a standard version ad risk being bypassed for the contract.
8. List 3 speaker options... see my [Speaker Packet sample on page 4](https://www.kevincsnyder.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Virtual-Speaker-Packet.pdf)
9. Be clear on your call to action. What do you want the viewer to do?
10. Align design style with your website and any other branding

> Needed elements in a One Page:

1. Headshot or 3 (different images)
2. Action photos \*
3. Branding - logo, coloring and overall style fits you
4. Testimonials \*
5. Bio - about the speaker (\< 100 words)
6. Sample clients (logo collage) \* - whether you've been paid by them or not, they were a client
7. Sample presentation topics
8. Sample presentation titles (no more than 4) - Module 2 in [*PAID to $PEAK*](/book)
9. Program descriptions for each title (strive for 50-75 words max - more info on your website) - Module 2 in [*PAID to $PEAK*](/book)
10. Call to action, what do you want them to do? (visit your website? watch your demo video? email you? submit on your website?)
11. List speaker options... see my [Speaker Packet sample on page 4](https://www.kevincsnyder.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Virtual-Speaker-Packet.pdf)

\* Speak to get these items.
