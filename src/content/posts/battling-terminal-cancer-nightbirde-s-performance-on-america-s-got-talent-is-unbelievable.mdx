As speakers, the ultimate way to connect with an audience is to be authentic and vulnerable.

Sharing your own "story" is one way, but you can also weave in stories from others.

I recently came across this story and added it to my "Speaker Notebook." I'll definitely be talking about Nightbirde in a future presentation.

In 2020, "Nightbirde" was diagnosed with terminal cancer and given six months to live. Her husband left two weeks later.

“We are so much more than any bad things that can happy to us though,” she said.

Nightbirde recently won the Golden Buzzer on America's Got Talent ("AGT"), but recently had to leave the competition because of her cancer.

In her first performance, she performed, "It's OK" - an original song she wrote describing this past year.

Although she left the competition, she's already won so many hearts and inspired so many with her words through fighting the hardest battle; to survive.

In a world so challenging, unfair and dark at times, light always overcomes. Struggles develop strength.

Nightbirde's strength and mindset exemplify beauty, grace and hope. She is living proof that we are so much greater than circumstance.

I hope you'll take a moment to watch her performance of "It's OK." Then share it with someone who might be going through a tough time right now.
