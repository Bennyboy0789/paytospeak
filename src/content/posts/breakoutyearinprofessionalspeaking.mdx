When I first started speaking, it was for free. I had no clue what I was doing. I didn't even know what I didn't know.

Speaking for me was just a hobby initially. I had a full-time job working on a college campus so my focus was not on getting PAID. A hobby income is what I earned too - zero. But I was fine with that.

Ironically though, I hired dozens of speakers for various student events at my college as well as various state and national events. In short, I was the "meeting planner." I knew what events hired speakers, how much they got paid, the contract process and the different topics and styles of speakers. And most importantly, I learned how to select the best speakers by finding the right ones.

Little did I know how important those experiences would be for me when the spark hit me one day and I realized there was more to speaking than meets the eye. If you watch my recent TEDx talk, you'll hear exactly the tipping point that changed my life.

<YouTube id="GbJaPqqV3A4" />

After a few years of speaking 1-2-3 times a month for other campuses and conferences across the country, I started making just as much money as my full-time job. This is when I started thinking seriously about launching into speaking full-time.

There's more to that story, but the essence of it is that I got more intentional and focused about learning the BUSINESS of speaking. I hired a coach from pulling out 401K money and it quickly paid for itself in dividends. I followed his advice, applied myself and the ROI manifested.

After a few years of successful speaking, other speakers started asking me questions, taking me out to lunch, buying me coffee, etc. I was happy to help others, but the time we spent together answering a few questions just was not enough for them. They needed more business guidance. There was so much to know. They also needed accountability to follow through.

**Step 1: Know what type of speaker you want to be (Module 1 focus inside PAID to $PEAK)**

There are many ways to speak and get PAID for speaking. And you can get PAID in different ways for each engagement. You just need to know the different types of speaker options and which you will pursue.

For example, do you envision becoming a keynote speaker, workshop trainer, retreat facilitator, emcee coach, consultant, all of the above?

Also, do you desire to speak and get PAID or is the exposure from speaking desirous because you have a training program, coaching or consulting business? I know many speakers who have many different business models. It's crucial for you to reflect on which speaking business model suits you best.

This is why Module 1 inside PAID to $PEAK will help you best.

**Step 2: Develop a proven presentation (Module 2 focus)**

What constitutes a presentation? A title, description, learning outcomes and bio gets you started. Most speakers don't have this. You don't even need presentation slides. I know extraordinary speakers who just require a microphone. However, I recommend at least a few slides to provide visual stimulation and intrigue that align with your content. Slide design is fairly simple.

What do I mean by proven presentation? Well, a presentation that is ready and referable. Nothing else matters if you're speech needs work.
