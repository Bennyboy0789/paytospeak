In this short video, I'm sharing a secret tip I discovered a few years back to make sure I never need to look behind me at the screen and turn my back on an audience.

This tip also gives me an advantage and edge over other speakers that has helped my speaking business, especially at conferences and receiving referrals and spin-off speaking opportunities.

When I present my keynotes and trainings, often times, the screen is behind me. Even when I request a "confidence monitor" in front of me and/or the stage, they often times forget or it's not done the right way.

> 1. NEVER turn your back on an audience.

> 2. ALWAYS know exactly where you are in your presentation.

> 3. Need to glance at notes occasionally? Here's how.

If you think you're on a presentation slide but the audience is looking at something different, then your entire presentation appears disorganized and you look unprofessional.

This happens frequently when remote clickers don't work due to interference... especially in large ballrooms with large audiences. Don't let this happen.

Speakers must control their environment and be set up for success. So in this video, I'm sharing with you how I use an HDMI extension cord and an audio extension cord to enable me to use my own laptop as my confidence monitor right in front of me.

This means I NEVER have to look behind me and turn my back on an audience. I know exactly where I'm at the entire time.
