While it's easy to feel frustrated over losing a $7,500 speaking engagement, there's much to be thankful for and proud of in having received the inquiry in the first place.

Let’s explore how this $7,500 speaking engagement came about, with the hope that these insights help you land similar speaking opportunities:

1. Having a Professional Website: The inquiry originated from my website, [KevinCSnyder.com](http://KevinCSnyder.com). [Check it out here.](https://kevincsnyder.com/) A well-crafted website acts as a key indicator of professionalism and credibility.
2. An Effective Contact Page: My Contact Page, [available here](https://kevincsnyder.com/hire-kevin/), played a crucial role. It not only allowed the inquirer to reach out but also provided key information, such as their speaker budget, which was $7,500.
3. Visibility Through Speaking Engagements: The inquirer had seen me speak previously. To increase your chances of landing speaking gigs, ensure you’re actively speaking. For tips on finding speaking opportunities, refer to Modules 2 and 3 in [*PAID to $PEAK*](https://www.amazon.com/How-Become-Professional-Speaker-SPEAK-ebook/dp/B083RD2TWS/ref=sr_1_1?crid=249YBMGCM3DP4&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.ZCepNDCy6UNMnaWhZ6165WAh20gtXDvLTCZ70C33cq1SnIUetiHGp6MZM-1aFNmVpyG4NyM2O7vnJ4HcEoP7LKCAi4r9DLnJueTRmyEY1ejX_QFSHWI-6Ii41MBfAL0j2p6Hc-Cy8_Bc6jjIA60_LGutx_w8QoKwtash2wAQU1ZcjG7I7ZPLKxo2NQ3ZG82KxALF4mE7_ae8kNiMt8LrAmAWXa7_FqzkKWxMIJs1Gus.9wwhWyz0jTSsPbNG0HmQpFVk8fK0r5vncQ6fqheFdwI&dib_tag=se&keywords=paid+to+speak&qid=1726255895&sprefix=paid+to+speak%2Caps%2C109&sr=8-1).
4. Utilizing Email Marketing and Lead Capture: The person who reached out was on my Mailchimp list, having opted in through a system called Talkadot, which captures emails and leads during my presentations.
5. Maintaining Communication with Past Audiences: As “they don’t need a speaker until they need a speaker,” staying in touch with past audiences is crucial. I communicate with them bi-weekly through Mailchimp.

**Lessons Affirmed:**

1. A Professional Website Is Essential: A speaker’s website is a cornerstone of their professional presence. Check out my website for an example of what works: [KevinCSnyder.com](http://KevinCSnyder.com).

3. Speak Often to Gain Referrals: The more you speak, the more referrals you’ll receive. A great speech will always be referred.
