Looking back on my speaking career, four (4) things I've done which I truly feel have helped me the most are:

- *Constant practice - repetition is the mother of skill*
- *Asking for feedback to improve - eliminate your blind spots*
- *Coaching to learn the 'process' - yes, there is a system for successful speaking*
- *Watching other speakers - you'll find your own unique style by discovering what you like and don't like*

**All four equally important... and I still apply them today, even after speaking for 20+ years.**
