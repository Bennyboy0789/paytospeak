Ever desire to pivot your professional speaking to also include middle and high school students, college students, educators and/or faculty?

The education space provides an exciting ocean of possibilities to equip, empower and transform our youth who need to hear your message to help prepare them for the real world out there. Professionals need to be equipped and empowered too.

Join this special virtual event to benefit the NSA Foundation where you'll hear from full-time professional speakers who STARTED their professional speaking in the youth, college and educator markets and continue to do so.

Our presenters span multiple NSA chapters and are volunteering their time to help you learn how to launch your speaking to students and educators at various levels.
