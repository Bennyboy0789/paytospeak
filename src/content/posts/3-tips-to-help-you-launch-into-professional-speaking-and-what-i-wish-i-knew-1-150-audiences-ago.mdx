**What is professional speaking?**

I personally define professional speaking as speakers who get PAID to $PEAK. Meaning, you are PAID to present in some form or fashion.

That could be keynotes, workshops, trainings, sales presentations, emcee'ing, ++...

IMPORTANT: this does not mean you need to aspire to be a full time professional speaker. You could supplement your current income and get PAID to $PEAK on the side like I did many moons ago until I was ready to launch full-time and quick my 'day job.'
