Three myths I want to crush up front about school and collegiate speaking first though:

**Myth** [**#1**](/blog/hashtags/1): Schools and colleges do not pay speakers.

**Fact** [**#1**](/blog/hashtags/1)**:** Says who? Yes they do, and many of my school/college speaking engagements pay much better than my corporate engagements. Be careful what assumptions you believe and who you take advice from.

**Myth** [**#2**](/blog/hashtags/2)**:** You have to be young to speak to youth.

**Fact** [**#2**](/blog/hashtags/2)**:** Wrong. Your life experience is what they crave and need to hear.

**Myth** [**#3**](/blog/hashtags/3)**:** There are not a lot of speaking opportunities at schools and colleges.

**Fact** [**#3**](/blog/hashtags/3)**:** Say hugh? There are over 4,000 colleges and universities in the United States alone, each of which have numerous opportunities to HIRE SPEAKERS every single semester.
