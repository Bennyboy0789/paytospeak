**Our charitable cause:** We are dedicating this event to support the ALS Foundation. Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis (ALS), more commonly known as Lou Gehrig’s disease, is a progressive neurodegenerative disease that destroys motor neurons, which are among the largest of all nerve cells in the brain and spinal cord. www.alsfoundation.org

The SPEAKER SHOWCASE is a joint collaboration between two Toastmasters clubs, SPEAKING PROFESSIONALLY TOASTMASTERS and BORN TOASTMASTERS. These two unique clubs are based in North Carolina and are affiliated with Toastmasters International which is comprised of over 16,000 clubs, 360,000 members and 145 countries.
