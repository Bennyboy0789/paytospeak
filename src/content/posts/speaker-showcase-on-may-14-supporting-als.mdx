**Our charitable cause:** We are dedicating this event to support the ALS Foundation. The SPEAKER SHOWCASE is a joint collaboration between two Toastmasters clubs, SPEAKING PROFESSIONALLY TOASTMASTERS and BORN TOASTMASTERS.
