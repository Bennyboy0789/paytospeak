## How to Land PAID Speaking Engagements & Learn More About the Accredited Speaker Designation

Have you ever wondered how some speakers turn their passion into **PAID speaking engagements**?

Or how to take your smaller speeches to bigger stages with more impact and opportunity?

Whether you are seeking more PAID speaking opportunities or are a Toastmaster seeking that next level in your speaking, join us for this exciting repeat session about the Accredited Speaker designation and PAID speaking engagements.

On **Thursday, November 20 at 12:00 p.m. EST**, Superior Speakers Advanced Toastmasters (SSA) is hosting me to deliver a **free webinar** open to *everyone* about the Accredited Speaker designation recognized by Toastmasters International. I am a member of SSA Toastmasters and honored to repeat this session in the spirit of helping other Toastmasters members (and aspiring speakers) earn the Accredited Speaker designation and how to earn PAID speaking engagements.

### What You’ll Learn

- **How to land PAID speaking engagements** using strategies I’ve developed over 20+ years as a professional speaker.
