One of the biggest challenges for aspiring speakers is figuring out who your target audience really is. Not just “anyone who needs motivation,” but an audience that:

- Has events where speakers are needed.
- Has a budget to pay speakers.

That’s exactly what Module 3 inside my book [*PAID to $PEAK*](/) is all about. I call it my Association Model and it's my roadmap and blueprint to how I started my speaking business and how I run my business to this day.

My Association Model provides you with clarity, direction, and momentum. It allows you to rinse and repeat your speech on a local, regional, and even national level, because associations are constantly looking for speakers. They run events, collect registration fees, and bring in sponsors, which means they have the structure and the budget to bring in professional speakers like you.

And I want you to know … this isn’t theory. This recent experience I am about to share with you is living proof.

**How I Launched My Speaking Business**

When I resigned my 'day job' as a Dean of Students to go full-time into speaking back in 2012, I started by focusing on ONE association. I remember sitting in a Greensboro, NC coffee shop, emailing every one of the 21 local chapters about speaking at one of their monthly meetings. It was a long caffeinated afternoon of research and emails - but it PAID off, literally.

My speaking mentor and coach is who guided me on which association to start with. Quite honestly, if I had picked the wrong association, I would be working at Home Depot right now. My coach was right.

Within 6 months, I had 11 speaking engagements. Most were unpaid or lightly paid … but I knew I had to put in the reps. And on my third keynote, someone in the audience happened to be on the state conference committee. They approached me afterward and invited me to speak at the state conference a few months later.

That single connection snowballed and became the ripple effect. Within a year, I had built a six-figure speaking business, simply by following the Association Model which I have outlined for you inside PAID to $PEAK, starting local, and working my way up.

**Why This Matters for You**

If you’re feeling stuck or overwhelmed about which audience to pursue, Module 3 inside PAID to $PEAK might be your needed roadmap. Associations give you:

- Structure (chapters, events, conferences),
- Opportunities (constant need for speakers), and
- Budget (thanks to registrations and sponsors).

Your job is to pick ONE association, start local, deliver value, and let your credibility and connections carry you forward.

As I like to say: If you chase more than one squirrel, you won’t catch any. Focus wins.
