Join NSA Carolinas and I this in Greensboro, NC for our annual ***Speaker Intensive Workshop*** to learn from professional speaking LEGENDS including our own Hall of Fame speakers, Cavett Award winners, past NSA Carolinas Presidents, Certified Speaking Professionals and more.

Attend to hear and glean their secrets for thriving as a professional speaker. Learn from them... not your own mistakes. I'll be there in attendance and cannot wait to hear their insights on how to further grow and accelerate.

This event is custom tailored for both seasoned speakers and those aspiring and emerging forward on the speaking journey. We’ve named it ***Speaker Intensive*** because it is just that, an intense educational and networking experience providing you with a unique blend of foundational sales mastery techniques and advanced strategies to position yourself ahead in the speaking profession.

**Below are just some highlights:**

- Our Speaker Intensive begins on Friday evening with a special event we’ve never done before, it’s an [“Evening of Art” hosted at William Mangum Fine Art](https://nsacarolinas.org/event-5467169) by NSA Carolinas member and renowned artist William Mangum. Be prepared for an amazing evening of connection w/ peers, wine, conversation amidst the beauty of Bill’s artwork and a special presentation from Bill himself. (*See bottom for Friday evening hotel room block code*)
- On Saturday, our Speaker Intensive continues at Transform GSO with the legendary Icon of Sales, Ray Leone, CPAE. Ray is Creator and Founder of the Original Sales Funnel ® and member of the Million Dollar Speaking Group. Ray will be sharing insights into how to convert one (1) speaking engagement into 6-figure retainer engagements.
- To close our Speaker Intensive, newly inducted Hall of Fame speaker, Jeff Civillico, CPAE, will be joining us from Las Vegas to share ‘*Best Practices: Before, During & After Events To Earn More* Engagements”

***Speaker Intensive*** also includes a Power Panel including legends **Lenora Billings-Harris (CPAE and Cavett Award Winner), Chuck Gallagher (CSP) and Stan Phelps (CSP).** This dynamite panel will dive in deep around sales mastery and tips to THRIVE as a professional speaker while also taking time to answer your questions. \
Some topics they’re poised to share insights include:

- How Best to Pitch Your Services
- Positioning & Branding
- Sales & Marketing
- Effective Networking
- 3rd Party Proof: Testimonials & Success Stories
- Current Landscape of the Professional Speaking Industry
- … and more.
